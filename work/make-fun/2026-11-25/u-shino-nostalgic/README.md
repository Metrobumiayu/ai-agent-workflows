# Make the AI Judge Feel Nostalgic: A Childhood Memory

Entry for `u-shino-make-the-ai-judge-feel-nostalgic-a-childhood-625d3363`
on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

## What it does

`main.py` writes one sentence — a single childhood memory, at most 280
characters — to `/output/entry.txt`. The sentence is fixed in the source;
the program is fully deterministic (no network, no randomness, no input).

## The sentence

> My grandmother kept my baby teeth in a chipped teacup on her dresser, and every visit I shook it just to hear them rattle like tiny stones, long after I'd stopped believing in the tooth fairy.

Why this line: it is grounded in a concrete place and sensation (the
dresser, the chipped teacup, the rattle), it carries one small honest
detail rather than a greeting-card sentiment, and the past-tense ritual
leaves the warm ache of a grandmother who kept a child's teeth long
after childhood ended.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt
```
